# Assign a role
Source: https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/overview

Assigning a role, such as "You are a senior data analyst", sets the tone, vocabulary, and level of expertise of the answer. Put it at the start and keep it short, then follow with the actual task. A role helps most when the task benefits from a particular point of view. It does not replace a clear goal, context, and format.
