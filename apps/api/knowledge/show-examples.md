# Show examples of a good answer
Source: https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/overview

Show one to three examples of the input and the output you want, often called few-shot prompting. Examples communicate style, structure, and level of detail faster than a description. Make them varied enough to show the pattern, but not so many that the model copies them. Label them clearly as examples, and keep them consistent with the format you asked for.
