# Say what to do, not only what to avoid
Source: https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/overview

Describe what you want the model to do rather than only what to avoid. "Write in plain sentences" works better than "do not use jargon", and "answer in two short paragraphs" beats "do not be long". Negative-only instructions leave the model guessing what to do instead. When something must be avoided, say so, and also state the alternative.
